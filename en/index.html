06 — Questions

FAQ

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

It is the practice of preparing a site's content and markup so AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews) understand it, cite it and recommend it in their answers.

Am I open to new opportunities?

Yes. I'm open to remote SEO, GEO and organic growth roles, as well as freelance web projects.

What kind of freelance web projects do I take on?

Corporate sites, portfolios and landing pages built with WordPress/Elementor, Webflow or static Astro sites, plus SEO/GEO audits and technical fixes on existing sites.

Where do I work from?

From Bogotá, Colombia, remotely with teams and clients across Latin America and the United States, in Spanish and English.
